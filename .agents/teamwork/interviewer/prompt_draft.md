## Requirements

### R1. Brand Positioning and Modern Neumorphic 2.0 Design System
Establish an intelligent, technologically sophisticated, engineering-driven identity for GS-Softwares using a Modern Neumorphism 2.0 aesthetic. Combine tactile UI components, subtle glass-like surfaces, neutral palette (background and surface `#E9EDF2`, primary text `#151A23`, secondary text `#687386`, accent `#526FE5`, dark contrast surface `#171C29`, success `#36A783`), purposeful depth and lighting (`box-shadow: 8px 8px 20px rgba(163, 177, 198, 0.38), -8px -8px 20px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with inset shadows for interactive elements), and Swiss-inspired typography (Space Grotesk headings, Inter body, IBM Plex Mono technical labels). Do not produce outdated repetitive card grids, excessive pill buttons, cartoonish illustrations, generic AI robot imagery, or low-contrast text.
